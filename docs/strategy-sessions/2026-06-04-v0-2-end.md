## Recommendation & Decision (Stages 6–7)

**Recommended and chosen: Path 1.** #28 is the top open tracked action and a
dangling reference the repo's own staleness check would flag; fixing it is the
highest-confidence move and a pre-req for everything else. Once gates are
runnable and templates are validated, CI can enforce them — compounding returns
on a repo that currently has zero automated enforcement. Path 2's SKILL
self-test harness is valuable but the largest unknown and sequences naturally
into v0.4.0 once a CI harness exists. Decision confirmed by the user in deep
mode.

**Directional change**: yes — establishes [ADR-0001](../adr/0001-executable-quality-gates.md)
(quality gates must be executable, not prose), the repo's first ADR.
